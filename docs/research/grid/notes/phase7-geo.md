## 9. How the override step works

`data/geo_overrides.csv`, one row per audited station: `sub, lat, lon, county, method, evidence, conf, replaces_method` as briefed, plus `action, osm_id, matched_name, old_county, old_conf`. Actions: `place` (pin site, OSM node and confidence; `match_method` becomes `override`), `unplace` (drop the matcher's site; the neighbour fill-in may place the station again with its own method), `demote` (keep the site, cap the confidence). Methods: `alt_site` 11, `name_partner` 13, `family_block` 13, `graph_adjacent` 2, `tpit_name` 2; `unplace_county` 18, `unplace_partner` 10; `demote_block` 2, `demote_partner` 1.
`geomatch.match_geo` calls `geo_overrides.apply_overrides` before the neighbour stage, so confident neighbours are judged against the corrected positions, and again after it, because that stage rewrites confidences. The override confidence is final; the second pass skips `unplace` so a legitimate fill-in survives. The table is validated on load (unique `sub`, known action and method, evidence required, coordinates in Texas, `NA` read as a code). Idempotent by test, and a fresh `match-geo` reproduces the committed `substations.csv`, `lines.csv` and `osm_substations.csv` byte for byte (checked three times, once by the opt-in test).
